Privacy

This website does not use advertising trackers, and it does not ask you for personal information.

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they read. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as the pages you view, your approximate location and the type of device you use. This information does not identify you personally. Google handles it under its own policies: see how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can turn it off with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

The games run entirely in your browser. Settings you save in a game (for example in the Pro edition) are stored only in your own browser and are never sent to us.

Purchases are handled by a third-party store. When you buy, that store processes your payment and contact details under its own privacy policy.

Anything you send me by email for a consultation is used only to reply to you, and is never shared with anyone else.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
