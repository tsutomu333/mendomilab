Why that matters

Toyota's shop-floor supervisors learned the Toyota Production System through experience. They can do it on the floor, but putting into words why it's done that way was someone else's job.

For about 10 years I asked them questions and talked things through with them, and organized and put into words the "why" of the Toyota Production System.

Turning what could only be passed on through experience into something that can be passed on in words — that is my greatest strength. The games I make now are that "why" given a shape.

What I'm good at

  • Making hard things easy to understand.
  • Rethinking from the premises, without "that's just how it is" or "we've always done it this way."
  • Working out, together with you, what the problem is and how it could be improved.
