For trainers

The games are built to be run by a facilitator. The guide tells you what to ask, when to stay quiet, and how to bring the lesson back to the shop floor.

Play first

Participants try their own ideas. Wrong answers are welcome — they are the material for the discussion.

Ask, don't tell

The guide gives you questions for each common mistake, so people find the answer themselves.

Praise, correct, watch over

Recognize good thinking, correct what needs correcting, and give people room to try again.

Take it back to work

Close with questions about the participants' own processes, so the session continues after the game ends.

Team licence: one organization, one facilitator, unlimited participants and sessions.

How it works
Tsutomu Takagi

Taught the Toyota Production System to former Toyota managers. 20 years at a Toyota group consulting company, where I trained about 50 trainers.

"For this boss, for this teammate, I'll go the extra mile."

Working alongside hundreds of former Toyota supervisors, I came to feel that the real source of Toyota's strength was mendomi — the trust that makes people say those words. Lean tools work when that trust is there.

Read the story →
mendomi (面倒見)

Caring about people at work and beyond, so that trust grows to the point where they think, "for this person, I'd do it." Praising, correcting and watching over are part of how that trust is built.
